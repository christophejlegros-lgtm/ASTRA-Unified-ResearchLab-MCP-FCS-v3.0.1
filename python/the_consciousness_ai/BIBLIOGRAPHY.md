# Bibliography of removed third-party documents

This vendored copy of [tlcdv/the_consciousness_ai](https://github.com/tlcdv/the_consciousness_ai)
previously carried full-text copies of third-party articles in two folders,
`info how to measure consciousness/` and `tech documentation/`. ASTRA does not
redistribute third-party publications, so those copies were removed from this
copy on 23 September 2026 and replaced by the references below. The upstream
project, its licence (`LICENSE.md`) and its attribution are otherwise unchanged.

Every DOI below was either printed on the removed document itself or resolved
against the DOI Foundation Handle system on 23 September 2026. arXiv identifiers
are taken from the removed files' own names.
